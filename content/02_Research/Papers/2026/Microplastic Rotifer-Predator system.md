# Trophic transfer and egestion dynamics of microplastics in the *Brachionus-Asplanchna* rotifer system

## Links

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s10661-026-15485-w
- ResearchGate: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/405164473_Trophic_transfer_and_egestion_dynamics_of_microplastics_in_the_Brachionus-Asplanchna_rotifer_system

## Main idea

Microplastics can move from prey rotifers (*Brachionus*) to predator rotifers (*Asplanchna*), but they are rapidly eliminated and did not show strong short-term biomagnification.

## Important points

- Fluorescent microplastics were ingested by both species.
- Trophic transfer between prey and predator was observed.
- Predators accumulated more particles initially.
- Most particles were eliminated within 24–48 h.
- Results suggest transient transfer rather than persistent accumulation.

## Why interesting

- Connects rotifer ecology with ecotoxicology.
- Useful model for studying contaminant movement in microscopic food webs.
- Shows difference between trophic transfer and biomagnification.
