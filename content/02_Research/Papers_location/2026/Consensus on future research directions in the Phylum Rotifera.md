## Main idea  
  
This preprint identifies future research priorities for the Phylum Rotifera using a Delphi process with international experts, producing a community-driven roadmap for advancing rotifer research.  
  
## Important points  
  
- Rotifers are ecologically important, but major knowledge gaps remain in their diversity, evolution, taxonomy and applied relevance.  
- The study used an online and in-person Delphi process during the 17th International Rotifer Symposium.  
- A total of 133 research questions were screened.  
- These were reduced to 100 questions for online scoring.  
- Sixty-seven questions advanced to an in-person workshop.  
- Final priority questions were classified into gold, silver and bronze categories.  
- Key themes included taxonomy, molecular tools, AI-assisted identification, phylogeny, ecological function, global change and biodiversity monitoring.  
- The study highlights the need to connect classical taxonomy with digital curation, DNA sequences, traits and computational tools.  
  
## Why interesting  
  
- Provides a collective research agenda for rotiferology.  
- Useful for students, researchers and funding proposals.  
- Identifies where the field has strong knowledge gaps.  
- Connects traditional rotifer taxonomy with modern approaches such as genomics, eDNA and AI-assisted identification.  
- Helps position rotifers as important organisms for ecology, evolution, biodiversity and global change research.
