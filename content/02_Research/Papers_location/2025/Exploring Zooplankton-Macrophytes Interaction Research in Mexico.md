## Main idea  
  
This chapter reviews the Mexican scientific literature on interactions between zooplankton and macrophytes using a bibliometric approach, highlighting research trends, knowledge gaps, and future opportunities in limnology and freshwater ecology.  
  
## Important points  
  
- The study focuses on Mexican literature related to zooplankton–macrophyte interactions.  
- It uses bibliometric analysis to identify publication patterns, dominant themes, authors, journals, and citation trends.  
- The chapter highlights the ecological importance of both zooplankton and aquatic plants in freshwater ecosystems.  
- It identifies research gaps related to macrophyte removal, food webs, allelopathy, anthropogenic stressors, and community-level interactions.  
- The work suggests that Mexico has strong potential for future research on limnological processes involving zooplankton and macrophytes.  
- It emphasizes the importance of collaboration and strategic editorial decisions for improving scientific visibility.  
  
## Why interesting  
  
- Connects Mexican limnology with bibliometric analysis.  
- Helps identify underexplored research areas in freshwater ecology.  
- Useful for planning future projects on zooplankton, aquatic plants, ecological interactions, and water quality.  
- Shows how bibliometric tools can guide scientific strategy and collaboration.
